SECTION 1 -- THE MASK
  Rollover exits fell from 48,859 (36.28%) to 27,250 (20.23%): the mask removed
  44% of them and touched nothing else. 48-bar time exits are unchanged at
  17,599, which is the correctness check -- the mask must only bite on short
  runways.
  Effective n fell 4,756 -> 4,311, i.e. 9.4%, against a 16.38% label loss. The
  masked labels were the most redundant ones in the sample.

SECTION 2 -- THE SHAPE OF THE TARGET
  The label distribution is BIMODAL, and this drives phase 7 model choice.
    60.3% of labels resolve at a barrier and take one of two near-deterministic
    values: -4.0138 (stop) or +5.9857 (target), each with sd 0.007. That sd is
    only the spread and slippage varying; the level is the barrier itself.
    39.7% are time or rollover exits and are continuously distributed,
    sd ~1.8-2.0 ATR.
  So predicting the SIGN of the net label is, for three fifths of the sample,
  predicting which barrier gets hit. Regression on the return would be
  dominated by the two spikes. This is the argument for classification.

  Both non-barrier buckets have POSITIVE means (long: time +1.1111, rollover
  +0.3100). That is a survivorship effect, not an edge: a path still alive at
  the horizon did not fall to -4 ATR, so it is conditioned upward. It does not
  survive the barrier-resolved mass, and the pooled long mean is -0.1315.

SECTION 3+4 -- BY YEAR
  Long win rate runs 39.80% (2023) to 49.87% (2025). Breakeven at 4/6 ATR with
  the measured c = 0.2002 is 42.0%. So 2021, 2023 and 2026 sit BELOW breakeven
  on the long side and 2024/2025 above -- which is the drift, and is why
  Section 5's always-long benchmark exists.
  eff/lab is 0.0369-0.0391 across all six years. Rev 1 declined to extrapolate
  a per-year effective n from the pooled ratio on the grounds that concurrency
  tracks ATR. Measured, it barely moves. The caution was warranted and the
  worry was not.

SECTION 5+6 -- FOLD COUNT, THE BENCHMARK, AND FOLD 0
  FOLD COUNT: the config key `walk_forward.n_blocks: 6` (renamed from n_folds
  on 2026-09-14) is the number of equal
  BLOCKS the history is cut into. Fold k trains on blocks 0..k-1 and tests on
  block k, for k = 1..5, so SIX BLOCKS PRODUCE FIVE FOLDS. Block 0 is a
  training seed only and is never a test window. Rev 1's prose was wrong and
  its table was right. The parameter name is misleading and should be read as
  n_blocks.

  ALWAYS-LONG: folds 2 and 3 pay +0.2161 and +0.2221 ATR per trade,
  uniqueness-weighted, for holding gold and nothing else. A model returning
  +0.15 ATR on fold 3 has LOST to buy-and-hold. Zero is not the bar.

  FOLD 0: the four 1y percentile-rank features are 100.0% null in fold 0's
  TRAINING window and 5.2% null in its TEST window. Not "essentially absent" --
  entirely absent. Fold 0 trains in a 54-feature space and is tested in a
  58-feature one. LightGBM routes every training row down the missing branch,
  so nothing it learned about those four can generalise. Fold 0 is reported
  separately and never averaged into a headline.
  The next largest train-minus-test null gap after those four is 2.2pp
  (volume_z_500 and the other 500-bar relatives). Immaterial.
