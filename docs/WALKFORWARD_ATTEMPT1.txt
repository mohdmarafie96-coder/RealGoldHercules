READING THE RUN
================================================================================

1. THE DEGENERATE THRESHOLD -- the proximate cause

   Inner-fold expectancies reached +3.3789 (fold 1 short), +2.4469 (fold 0
   short) and +1.1484 (fold 4 short). No trading rule on this label set earns
   +3.38 ATR per trade. Those are the argmax of ~100 candidate thresholds over
   a noisy inner sample, landing on a handful of inner-validation winners.

   select_threshold maximises expectancy with only a min_take=30 floor. On
   inner folds of roughly 10,000 rows that is 0.3% -- effectively no
   constraint. The selector therefore degenerates toward the most extreme
   threshold it can find, and coverage collapses.

   The 20% coverage floor was pre-registered in 0.2i as a REPORTING rule. It
   was never wired into 0.2j as a SELECTION constraint. Attempt 1 is the
   consequence.

2. THE UNTRANSFERABLE THRESHOLD -- a second, independent defect

   The threshold is chosen as an ABSOLUTE probability on inner-validation
   predictions, then applied to outer-test predictions from a DIFFERENT model
   -- one refitted on the full outer training window. Those two probability
   distributions are not calibrated to each other, so the same numeric
   threshold selects a different quantile on each.

   Fold 2 is the clearest case: threshold 0.6143 took 32 of 18,771 bars, 0.2%.
   Fold 4: 0.6454 took 14 bars, 0.1%.

   This defect is independent of defect 1 and would survive fixing it.

3. WHAT THE GUARDS DO AND DO NOT SAY

   SHUFFLED-LABEL CONTROL is the one guard that returns usable information.
   Shuffled training targets give Wnet -0.4795; the real run gives -0.0863.
   The real run beats its own shuffled control by 0.39 ATR. THE PIPELINE IS
   NOT LEAKING, and the models are not pure noise. At 1.6% coverage this is
   not evidence of an edge, but it does rule out the failure mode the guard
   exists to detect.

   TIME-OF-DAY ABLATION cannot be read. Dropping the session features moved
   coverage from 1.6% to 0.7%; the -0.4037 delta is a comparison between two
   samples of 68 and 29 effective observations. Inconclusive, not negative.

   COST-REGIME SPLIT cannot be read. Folds 1-2 +0.0857 against folds 3-4
   -0.6495 looks like a cost story and is not: folds 2 and 4 took 32 and 14
   trades. This is sampling noise wearing a pattern.

   GEOMETRY ROBUSTNESS cannot be read, and the 6.0/9.0 row is actively
   misleading: +0.5167 at 0.1% coverage is a handful of trades. It MUST NOT be
